I'm Hosting Open Mic Host at SouthernEaze

August 12, 2025

I have the great honor of assuming hosting duties at the very popular Open Mic at SouthernEaze Bar and Grill in Lutz. This open mic flourished for years under the direction of our good friend Steven West. Steve is handing the baton, oops, clipboard, to me.

It is a real privilege to be able to host this event. Open mic occurs every other Wednesday (see my Events page for the next date!).

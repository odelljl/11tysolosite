I have the great honor of assuming hosting duties at the very popular Open Mic
at [SouthernEaze Bar and Grill](https://southerneaze.com/) in Lutz. This open
mic flourished for years under the direction of our good friend Steven West.
Steve is handing the baton, oops, clipboard, to me.

It is a real privilege to be able to host this event. Open mic occurs every
other Wednesday (see my [Events]() page for the next date!).
